### Step 1: Pain Discovery (`skills/pain-point-research`)
* **Natural Complaint Queries Crawled:**
  * *"I've tried vinegar, CLR, bleach... nothing works on the shower glass."*
  * *"Tvrdá voda v Praze mi úplně zničila sprchový kout, po 2 týdnech je sklo bílé a matné."*
  * *"Savo smells so toxic and just drips down vertical tiles without cleaning."*
* **1–3★ Review Mining (Pain within Pain):**
  * Existing spray cleaners drip down vertical glass without soaking.
  * Pumice stones crumble into abrasive powder and **scratch expensive glass**.
  * Steel wool strips the chrome coating off bathroom faucets.
* **Solution Gap:** A chemical-free, scratch-free physical rubber block that removes mineral scale on contact using only tap water.
